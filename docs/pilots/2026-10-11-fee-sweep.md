# Lean fee sweep — 2026-10-11

Goal: a fee and customer configuration that teaches the lesson in a class of 50–80 students
(26 and 40 bot teams), 15-minute rounds (bots at 10×, 90 s window), hash classifier (no Azure).

Small = 26 teams (6 per persona + 2 Balanced (holds)); large = 40 teams (9 per persona + 4 holds).
Customers are set per team (e.g. 15/team → 390 or 600). Per-run reports are in `sweep/`.

From D1 on, bot Marketing spends 12 class-seconds reading and titling each joke (commit 972e541),
so a batch of 10 waits on the desk about three times as long as a batch of 3, as with people.

## Recommendation

**E4**: shipping $1 / $2 / $3, storage 5¢, hosting 5¢, listing 10¢, discard 1¢; buy threshold 8,
preference base 5, budget $3; **18 customers per team** (set from the team count). Across both class sizes:
Learner (batches of 3) earns about twice Spray's profit in Round 2; batches of 3 reach a first sale in
~14 s vs ~36 s for batches of 10; holding batches loses money in both rounds; 57–69 % of teams are in
profit; Spray's Round 1 fees are 56–66 % of revenue. E3 (15/team) is the stricter alternative.

Caveat: bot joke quality is a hash, not real humour. The buy threshold must be checked against real
Azure-scored jokes in a small human trial before class.

## Update: 20 teams (the real class limit), paired contrast

Earlier runs used a fixed joke seed per team index, so repeat runs gave each team the same jokes and the
holding contrast mixed holding with joke luck (commit 7e83eab fixes this: each Balanced (holds) team draws
its Balanced partner's jokes, and every run uses a fresh seed). Rerun at 20 teams (4 per persona + 4 holds):
